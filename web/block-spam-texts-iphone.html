How to stop spam texts on iPhone

Start with the switch Apple already gave you. If that is not enough, a message filter sorts by content instead of by sender. Here is what each one can and cannot reach.

Junk text filtering is part of Nixring Pro. Free download to start.

Step one, no app needed

Switch on Filter Unknown Senders

  1. Settings, then Messages

    Scroll to the message filtering section.

  2. Turn on Filter Unknown Senders

    Messages from anyone not in your contacts, and who you have never replied to, move into a separate list.

  3. Silence what is left

    In Settings, Notifications, Messages, Customize Notifications, switch off notifications for unknown senders. The messages still arrive; they stop interrupting you.

For many people this is the whole answer, and it is free. Try it for a week before paying anyone for anything.

What it will not do. It sorts by who sent the message, not by what the message says. A parcel delivery scam and a real two-factor code from a bank both come from unknown senders, so both land in the same pile. And once you reply to a number even once, its later messages come straight back to your inbox.

Step two

What a message filter extension does differently

iOS lets one app at a time act as an SMS filter. When a message arrives from a sender you have never written to, iOS passes the sender and the body to that app inside a sandbox, waits for one of three verdicts, and then files the message. Nixring answers with junk, promotion, or leave it alone.

The classifier runs offline and looks at three things:

  • 28 scam phrases. The wording that carries a smishing attempt, such as asking you to verify an account, warning that a parcel could not be delivered, or announcing a prize.
  • Links. Every host in the message is extracted, including addresses written without a protocol. 10 known link shorteners and 16 domain endings that scam campaigns rely on send a message straight to Junk. An optional aggressive mode treats any link at all as junk.
  • 8 promotional words. Sales and discount wording gets the softer promotion verdict, which files the message without calling it a scam.

A whitelist of names, a keyword you would like added, or a per-sender exception are not available. The lists above are fixed in the app; the only switch you control is aggressive link mode.

Limits worth knowing before you pay

Three things no SMS filter on iPhone can do

  • It never sees messages from senders already in your Messages history. iOS only routes unknown senders to a filter.
  • It cannot delete a message, reply to it, or report it to a carrier. The only outcome is which folder the message lands in.
  • It cannot learn from your corrections, because it is never told whether its verdict was right.

A message wrongly filed as junk is still in the Junk folder in Messages, so nothing is lost. It is worth checking that folder for the first week.

Questions

Frequently asked

How do I stop spam texts on iPhone without an app?

Open Settings, Messages, and switch on Filter Unknown Senders. iOS then moves every message from a sender who is not in your contacts and who you have never replied to into a separate Unknown Senders list, and stops the notifications. This is built into iOS, costs nothing, and is the first thing to try.

What does a Message Filter app add on top of that?

Apple's own filter sorts by whether it knows the sender. A filter extension sorts by what the message says, so a scam text lands in Junk while a two-factor code from an unknown short code can stay in your inbox. Both mechanisms run at the same time.

Does Nixring read my text messages?

iOS hands the extension the sender and body of a single message from an unknown sender, in a sandbox with no network access, asks for a verdict, and discards it. Nixring stores nothing and sends nothing. Messages from people already in your Messages history are never shown to any filter extension at all.
