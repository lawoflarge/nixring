# Nixring Pricing

Nixring: Spam Call Blocker — iOS (iPhone), iOS 17+.

## Free
- Price: $0 to download and use.
- Includes: spam and scam call blocking with a built-in, on-device blocklist of known bad numbers, and a limited number of your own custom blocked numbers.
- No account, no sign-up, no tracking.

## Nixring Pro
- Price: optional subscription. $4.99 per week with a 3-day free trial, or $29.99 per year.
- Either plan grants Pro; both auto-renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and can be managed or canceled any time in App Store settings.
- Unlocks: junk and scam text filtering, an auto-updating spam blocklist, a whitelist for real callers, prefix and range call rules, unlimited blocked numbers, and full protection stats.

## Notes
- Spam call blocking with the built-in blocklist is free; Nixring Pro is optional.
- Prices are set in USD; the App Store shows local-currency equivalents.
- Source of truth: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6792562928
