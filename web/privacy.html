Privacy Policy

Effective 20 July 2026

Short version: Nixring runs entirely on your device. It collects nothing, uploads nothing, and sells nothing. There are no accounts and no servers.

1. Our approach

Nixring: Spam Call Blocker ("Nixring", "the app", "we") is built to protect your privacy by design. The entire purpose of the app is to block unwanted calls and texts without your personal information ever leaving your iPhone.

2. Information we do not collect

We want to be completely clear about what Nixring does not do:

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit your contacts.
  • We do not collect your call history or the phone numbers that call or text you.
  • We do not read the content of your calls or messages.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising SDKs, trackers, or third-party data brokers.
  • We do not require an account, email address, or login.

3. How blocking works on-device

Nixring uses Apple's Call Directory and Message Filter extensions. Nixring provides iOS with a blocklist of known spam and scam numbers, and iOS itself performs the blocking. This all happens locally on your device inside Apple's secure system framework. The numbers that call or text you are handled by iOS and are never seen by us or sent anywhere.

4. Data stored on your device

The spam blocklist and your app settings (for example, which categories you have enabled) are stored locally on your iPhone. This data stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app. It is never uploaded.

5. Subscriptions and payments

Nixring offers an optional auto-renewing subscription. All purchases and subscription management are handled by Apple's App Store and StoreKit. We never see or store your payment details. Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Children's privacy

Nixring does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. Because the app collects no personal data at all, it poses no data-privacy risk to users of any age.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date above. Material changes will be reflected in an app update.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email levin.schwab@gmx.de and we will be happy to help.
