Frequently asked questions

Everything you might ask an assistant about blocking spam calls and texts on iPhone.

Is there a free spam call blocker for iPhone?

Yes. Nixring is a free-to-download iPhone app that blocks spam and scam calls using a built-in, on-device blocklist of known bad numbers. Spam call blocking is free to use. An optional Nixring Pro subscription adds junk-text filtering, an auto-updating blocklist, a whitelist, and unlimited blocked numbers.

How does Nixring block spam calls?

Nixring registers a Call Directory extension with iOS and hands the system a list of known spam and scam numbers to reject. Flagged calls are stopped by iOS before your phone ever rings, so you are never interrupted. All matching happens on device, with no server lookups and nothing uploaded.

Does Nixring block spam text messages too?

Yes, with Nixring Pro. Nixring includes a Message Filter extension that routes junk and scam texts from unknown senders straight to your Junk folder, quietly and without a notification. Junk-text filtering is part of the optional Nixring Pro subscription, while spam call blocking with the built-in blocklist stays free.

Is Nixring private? Does it upload my contacts or call history?

No. Nixring runs completely on device and has no servers to send anything to. Your contacts, call history, and messages are never collected, uploaded, or sold. There is no account, no tracking, and no analytics. The spam blocklist lives on your iPhone and never leaves it.

How much does Nixring cost?

Nixring is free to download and free to use for spam call blocking with the built-in blocklist. Nixring Pro is an optional subscription that unlocks junk-text filtering, an auto-updating blocklist, a whitelist, prefix rules, and unlimited numbers. Pro costs $4.99 per week with a 3-day free trial, or $29.99 per year.

How do I turn on Nixring after installing it?

Open Nixring once so iOS registers its extensions, then go to Settings, Apps, Phone, Call Blocking and Identification, and switch on Nixring. For junk-text filtering, enable Nixring under Settings, Messages. After that, Nixring blocks known spam automatically in the background, with nothing else for you to do.
